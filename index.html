Please read the instructions carefully before starting the examination:

1. General Instructions:

  1. The total duration of the examination is displayed on the countdown timer. The clock will be set at the server and the countdown timer at the top right corner of your screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the exam.
  2. When the timer reaches zero, the examination will end by itself. You will not be required to end or submit your examination.
  3. In Banking & Regulatory exams (SBI PO, IBPS PO, IBPS SO), sectional timing is strictly enforced. You cannot navigate to other sections until the current section time has elapsed or you confirm section submission. Once a section is submitted, it is locked and you cannot revisit it.

Section # Section Name Total Questions Max Marks Time (Minutes) Language

3. Question Palette Symbol Definitions:

The question palette displayed on the right side of the screen will show the status of each question using one of the following official symbols:


1		 Not Visited: You have not visited the question yet. (Not evaluated for marks)

2		 Not Answered: You have not answered the question. (Not evaluated for marks)

3		 Answered: You have answered the question. (Evaluated for marks: +1.00 or -0.25)

4		 Marked for Review: You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review. (Not evaluated for marks)

5		 Answered Marked for Review: You have answered the question, but marked it for review. (EVALUATED for marks as per official banking rules)

4. Navigating to a Question:

  1. Click on the question number in the Question Palette at the right of your screen to go to that question directly. Note that using this option does NOT save your answer to the current question.
  2. Click on Save & Next to save your answer for the current question and then go to the next question.
  3. Click on Mark for Review & Next to save your answer if selected, mark it for review, and go to the next question.
  4. To deselect your chosen answer, click on the button Clear Response.

5. Negative Marking Scheme:

There will be penalty for wrong answers marked in the Objective Tests. For each question for which a wrong answer has been given by the candidate, one fourth or 0.25 of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted as penalty to arrive at corrected score. If a question is left blank, i.e. no answer is marked by the candidate, there will be no penalty for that question.
